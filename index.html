Interactive engineering demo — simulated data.

An aseptic vial filling line: vials are sterilised in a hot-air tunnel, filled and weighed, stoppered inside the RABS, crimp-capped, labelled and inspected, and packed into serialised trays. Rejects are diverted automatically. It runs entirely in your browser.

  1. Tick the line clearance checklist in the Batch panel, then press Reset and Start.
  2. In the Fault injection panel, open the RABS door or let the filling pump drift, and watch the hold or the fill-weight rejects.
  3. When a tray is full the line suspends; press Exchange tray and it resumes by itself.
  4. Acknowledge the alarms, recover with Unhold or Clear and Reset. When the planned vials are filled or the bulk tank T1 runs empty, the line runs empty and goes to Complete; then close the batch to see its summary.

Simulation only. It does not connect to or control any equipment and is not a validated production, safety or GMP system. Terms such as line clearance, batch record and audit trail are shown in simplified form, and the state model is a simplified example in the style of S88/PackML.

View state logic (SCL sketch) 
// Abort, hold and suspend causes are evaluated every scan
IF #EStop OR #PumpFault THEN #State := ABORTING;
ELSIF (#RabsDoorOpen OR #DpLow OR #TunnelLow OR #RejectLimit OR #StopperEmpty)
      AND #State = EXECUTE THEN #State := HOLDING;
ELSIF #TrayFull AND #State = EXECUTE THEN #State := SUSPENDING;   // waiting, not a fault
END_IF;
CASE #State OF
  STOPPED:   IF #Cmd.Reset AND #LineClearanceOK THEN #State := RESETTING; END_IF;
  IDLE:      IF #Cmd.Start AND #Auto THEN #State := STARTING; END_IF;
  EXECUTE:   IF #VialsIn >= #VialsToFill OR #BulkLow THEN #State := COMPLETING; END_IF;
  HELD:      IF #Cmd.Unhold AND NOT #HoldCause THEN #State := UNHOLDING; END_IF;
  SUSPENDED: IF NOT #TrayFull AND NOT #HoldCause THEN #State := UNSUSPENDING; END_IF;   // machine-generated Unsuspend
  COMPLETING:IF #LineEmpty THEN #State := COMPLETE; END_IF;
  COMPLETE:  IF #Cmd.Reset AND #BatchClosed AND #LineClearanceOK THEN #State := RESETTING; END_IF;
  ABORTED:   IF #Cmd.Clear AND #AllAcked THEN #State := CLEARING; END_IF;
  // ... STARTING, HOLDING, UNHOLDING, SUSPENDING, UNSUSPENDING, STOPPING, CLEARING
END_CASE;
